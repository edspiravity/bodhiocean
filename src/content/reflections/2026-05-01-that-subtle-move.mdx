When a sound is heard—<br />
almost instantly,<br />
it becomes “something”.

When a feeling arises—<br />
it is already<br />
“mine”.

When a thought appears—<br />
it is followed,<br />
as if it must lead somewhere.

So quick,<br />
it is rarely caught<br />
in attention.

The grasping does not announce itself.<br />
Yet it leans us forward.

Reaching for what is pleasant.<br />
Pushing away what is not.<br />
The subtle act of holding<br />
what was never stable.

And with it,<br />
a center seems to appear.

As someone who prefers.<br />
As someone who resists.<br />
As someone who owns.

But look closely.

Is the grasping done by someone?<br />
Or does the “someone” appear<br />
with the grasping?

When the movement quiets—

nothing to hold,<br />
nothing being held—

what remains?

## Reflection

At any moment today—

notice the instant<br />
something is liked or disliked.

Before the story forms,<br />
is there a tiny movement?

Before a thought is followed,<br />
is there a leaning<br />
towards or away?

Just notice.

## BodhiOcean

We are not here to accumulate ideas.<br />
We are here to loosen what is unnecessary.<br />
The ocean is already vast.<br />
We are only learning not to cling to the shore.
