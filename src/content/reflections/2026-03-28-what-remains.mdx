When the still ocean meets the wind,<br />
The waves and droplets form,<br />
And they drop<br />
Right back to the ocean

Where went the waves and droplets?<br />
Were they waves and droplets first?<br />
Or they were just water<br />
But in the form of waves and droplets?

When thoughts arise, a thinker is form<br />
When feelings arise, a feeler is form<br />
When knowing arise, a knower is form<br />
But when they fade off, where do they all go to?

There is no owner in the body.<br />
No thinker behind the thoughts.<br />
No feeler within the feelings.<br />
No knower apart from knowing.

And yet

Experience continues.

Thoughts still arise.<br />
Feelings still move.<br />
Seeing still happens.<br />
Knowing still happens.

If there is no fixed “I” to be found,<br />
what is it that remains?

Let's just let it in bare<br />
Without any idea.<br />
Or a need to be named.

What is already here<br />
Before calling anything “me”?

Is there a center?

Or is there simply<br />
a field of experience,<br />
appearing and disappearing<br />
on its own?

Sit quietly.

Nothing needs to be removed.<br />
Nothing needs to be added.

Without reaching for an “I”,<br />
what is lacking?

## Reflection

When the next experience unfolds —<br />
a sound, a thought, a feeling —

pause, just for a moment.

Not to analyse.<br />
Not to follow the story.

Just let it be,<br />
and notice:

Does anything need to hold it?

When a thought appears,<br />
is there someone producing it?

When a feeling moves,<br />
is there someone inside it?

Or does it simply arise,<br />
known,<br />
and pass?

Let attention rest,<br />
without searching for a center.

Just this —<br />
happening.

Without naming,<br />
without claiming,<br />
without grasping,

sit with that.

Not to arrive at an answer,<br />
or to sleep at it,<br />
but to see<br />
what has never been absent.
___

## BodhiOcean

We are not here to accumulate ideas.<br />
We are here to loosen what is unnecessary.<br />
The ocean is already vast.<br />
We are only learning not to cling to the shore.
