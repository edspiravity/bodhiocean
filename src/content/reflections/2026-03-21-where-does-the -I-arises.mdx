title: "Where does the \"I\" arises?"
date: "2026-03-21"
tags: ["Buddhism", "Awakening", "Suffering", "Self-Inquiry", "Foundations"]

I think.<br />
I decide.<br />
I feel.<br />
I perceive.

I know all these<br />
because I am conscious.

I know when I am awake.<br />
I know when I have fallen asleep.

Even in dreams,<br />
I see and feel things<br />
as though they are real.

I relive the past,<br />
recalling memories —<br />
happy, sad, or angry.

I imagine the future,<br />
anticipating what may come —<br />
excitement, worry, or plans.

And yet,

Where is this “I”?

Where is the one<br />
that thinks, feels, perceives,<br />
and knows?

Suddenly this question<br />
rocks my world.

Is it because there is a physical body<br />
that there must be an owner?

Is it because thinking occurs<br />
that there must be a thinker?

Is it because feelings arise<br />
that there must be someone who feels?

Is it because perception appears<br />
that there must be a perceiver?

Is it because knowing happens<br />
that there must be a knower?

But where are they?

Where is the owner,<br />
the thinker,<br />
the perceiver,<br />
the knower?

Was the idea of an “I”<br />
constructed by the mind<br />
to answer this question?

Or perhaps it arose<br />
because clinging appeared —

Clinging to the body,<br />
to thinking,<br />
to feeling,<br />
to perceiving,<br />
to knowing.

And so an “I” was formed.

But then—

Can there be a body<br />
without an owner?

Thinking<br />
without a thinker?

Feelings<br />
without someone who owns them?

Perception<br />
without a perceiver?

Knowing<br />
without a knower?

I do not rush to answer.

I simply look.

Where does this “I”<br />
actually arise?

## Reflection

At any moment,<br />
there seems to be an “I” at the center of experience.

An “I” that thinks,<br />
that feels,<br />
that knows.

It is just there,<br />
without question.

But if we pause and ask—

When a thought arises,<br />
is there truly a thinker behind it?

Or does the thought simply appear?

When a feeling is present,<br />
is there someone who feels it?

Or is it just felt?

When seeing happens,<br />
is there a seer apart from what is seen?

Or is there only seeing?

Look carefully.

Right now—

Can the “I” be located<br />
in the body?

In the thoughts?

In the feelings?

In the act of knowing itself?

Or are these simply movements<br />
that arise and pass,<br />
while the mind gathers them<br />
and calls them “me”?

Sit a while,<br />
and let everything flow.

Stay with the experience.

## BodhiOcean

We are not here to accumulate ideas.<br />
We are here to loosen what is unnecessary.<br />
The ocean is already vast.<br />
We are only learning not to cling to the shore.
